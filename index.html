Microgreens in everyday food

Twelve simple recipes that use fresh wheat, ragi, horsegram (kollu) and green gram (pasi payir) microgreens, from dosa and rasam to smoothies and wheat juice. Grown on a tray in a week, ready to go into your next meal.

Using microgreens well

  • Wash well with clean water before you use them.
  • Use them before the expiry date on the pack.
  • Add them at the end of cooking, so they stay green and crunchy.
  • Keep them in the fridge in a dry box with.
  • Chop wheat greens fine or blend them, as they are a bit tough.
  • Do not eat them if they look slimy, smell bad or turn yellow.
